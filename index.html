About the Founder

A physician who believes the best medicine is the one you never need

Dr Ajoy Kumar Paul founded AJOYVIDA — Dr Ajay Social Wellness & Life Solutions — with one conviction: lasting health is not prescribed, it is cultivated. His practice blends modern medical understanding with time-tested lifestyle wisdom to help people reverse the trajectory of chronic disease before it begins.

"We don't treat reports. We treat the whole human being — mind, body, emotion, relationships and spirit — so that medicine becomes the support, not the dependency."

Through consultations, structured programs and large-scale social awareness work, he and his team are taking preventive health to families, schools, and workplaces — one life at a time.

The MPESS 5-Pillar Approach

Five pillars. One complete human being.

Most care treats one dimension of a person. The AJOYVIDA MPESS framework restores balance across all five — because real, lasting wellness stands on every pillar at once.

M

Mental

Clarity, focus and resilient thought patterns through cognitive habits, mindfulness and mental-fitness practices.

Train the mind
P

Physical

Nutrition, movement, sleep and metabolic strength — the biological foundation on which every other pillar rests.

Strengthen the body
E

Emotional

Recognising, expressing and releasing emotions in healthy ways — from grief and anger to joy and gratitude.

Balance the heart
S

Social

Meaningful relationships, community bonds and the healing power of connection, service and belonging.

Connect with people
S

Spiritual

Purpose, values and inner stillness — the quiet centre that gives direction and peace to everything else.

Awaken the spirit

"When all five pillars stand strong, disease loses its ground — and medicine finds its rest."

Social & Health Awareness

Wellness beyond the clinic — for the community

AJOYVIDA carries preventive health education to where life actually happens — schools, streets, workplaces and villages — advancing the Mission of a Medicine Free India.

Health Camps & Screenings

Free community camps for blood-pressure, blood-sugar and lifestyle-risk screening, with on-the-spot counselling and referral pathways.

School & College Sessions

Interactive sessions for young minds on nutrition literacy, screen-life balance, stress skills and building healthy habits early.

Village & Slum Outreach

Door-to-door wellness education, hygiene and nutrition guidance for underserved communities, delivered with local volunteers.

Public Awareness Drives

Rallies, exhibitions and observation-day events on diabetes, obesity, mental health and the power of preventive living.

Workplace Wellness Talks

Executive health briefings and lunch-and-learn sessions that turn office culture into a partner of employee wellbeing.

Medicine-Free Lifestyle Workshops

Hands-on workshops teaching food-as-medicine cooking, breathwork and daily-rhythm design — the core of Mission: Medicine Free India.
